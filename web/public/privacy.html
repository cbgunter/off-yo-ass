Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026

Off Yo Ass is a personal, single-user application built and operated by its one user. It is not offered as a public service and does not have other users or accounts.

What it reads

With the account holder's own authorization, the app reads health and activity data from Garmin Connect, calendar availability from Google Calendar (read-only), and public weather forecasts from the National Weather Service.

Where it's stored

All data is stored in the account holder's own private AWS account and database. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising, and is not accessible to anyone but the account holder.

Third-party processing

Some data is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate the app's daily recommendations. It is used only to produce that response and is governed by Anthropic's own privacy policy.
